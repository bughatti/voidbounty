# VoidBounty - now part of VoidHonor

**Works on:** WoW Forever

**VoidBounty has moved into [VoidHonor](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/voidhonor).** Everything it did is still there, in VoidHonor's **World PvP** pages:
- **The nearby warning**: the red box when enemy players are around, red lines and a sound for players who killed you
- **Blood debts**: who killed you, how often, where
- **Sightings and tracking**: Danger here, enemy players seen by you and your side, Track a player
- **Most wanted**: who's killing your side this week
- Sharing with your side works as before, with players still on VoidBounty too

VoidHonor adds the rest of WoW Forever PvP around it: your rank and this week's Rank Points cap, the Field of Honor weeklies, Honor, and what every battleground really gave you. Inside a battleground, a box shows the score and who holds what and has one-click calls (Inc, Inc 3+, Need help). The **Honor shop** shows what your Honor can buy for your class, and where.

**[Get VoidHonor on CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/voidhonor)**

## Moving over
1. Install **[VoidHonor](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/voidhonor)** from CurseForge. **Keep VoidBounty installed for now.**
2. Log in. Each character's blood debts, sightings and settings move over by themselves the first time that character logs in with both installed.
3. Once every character that used VoidBounty has logged in once, you can remove VoidBounty.

This version of VoidBounty does nothing else: it keeps your saved data until VoidHonor has taken it, and tells you where things went.
